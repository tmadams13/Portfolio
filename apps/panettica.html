Overview

Panettica is a desktop app for managing early-stage baking businesses, designed to simplify pricing, cost tracking, and sales analysis. Users can enter ingredients and prices to automatically calculate recipe costs, set sales prices, and review sales trends by item or category, enabling better operational decisions with minimal effort.

Features

  • Supports early-stage business planning
  • Automated calculation of recipe costs and pricing
  • Sales tracking with item-level and category-level summaries
  • Insights into top-selling recipes and trends over time
  • Intuitive interface for easy data entry and reporting
